# GMGN AI Screening & Smart Money Trading Skill

This skill defines the screening criteria and smart money tracking standards powered by **GMGN AI (https://gmgn.ai)** for **Solana, Base, Ethereum, and BSC**.

---

## 1. GMGN Smart Money & Sniper Audit Criteria

Before generating any high-confidence meme call or executing an entry:

### A. Smart Money & Whale Tracking
- **Smart Money Inflow:** Verify at least 2+ verified GMGN Smart Trader wallets have bought in the last 15 minutes.
- **Top Trader Win Rate:** Ensure average 30-day win rate of buying wallets is >= 60%.

### B. Insider & Sniper Risk Control
- **Sniper Holding Ratio:** Total token supply held by snipers MUST be **< 15%**.
- **Dev Holding Ratio:** Dev wallet holding MUST be **<= 10%** (or burned/renounced).
- **Wash Trading Check:** Reject tokens where > 30% of 24h volume is generated by fake/wash trading bots.
