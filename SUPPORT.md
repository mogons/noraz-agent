# Getting Support for Opencatz AI

Welcome to **Opencatz AI (Robinhood Chain Edition)**! Here are the official resources and channels to get help, report issues, or connect with the community.

---

## 💬 Interactive Support Channels

- **Discord Community & Support**: Join our Discord server at [https://discord.gg/5HMy95ZHuY](https://discord.gg/5HMy95ZHuY) and navigate to `#opencatz-control-room` for natural language chat, setup assistance, contributor coordination, and strategy configuration.
- **Official X (Twitter)**: Follow [@pxidentities](https://x.com/pxidentities/) for release announcements, alpha insights, and ecosystem updates.
- **Official Web Portal**: Browse [https://opencatz.xyz](https://opencatz.xyz) and technical docs at [https://opencatz.xyz/docs](https://opencatz.xyz/docs).
- **Telegram Notification Bridge**: Follow real-time signal broadcasts and status alerts.
- **Terminal TUI Diagnostics**: Run `opencatz doctor` or `opencatz terminal` in your terminal for real-time diagnostic checks on API keys, RPC connections, and wallet configuration.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If you encounter unexpected behavior, RPC connection issues, or have ideas for new sub-agents or Web3 features:

1. **Check Documentation**: Review our [README.md](README.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
2. **Search Issues**: Check if your issue has already been reported in the [GitHub Issue Tracker](https://github.com/dizcorvus/opencatz-ai-robinhood-chain/issues).
3. **Submit an Issue**: Provide detailed steps to reproduce, execution mode (`DRY_RUN`, `AUTO_EXECUTE`), Node.js version, and relevant log snippets.
